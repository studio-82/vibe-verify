What Vibe Verify stores

Vibe Verify is designed to keep review evidence on your Mac. Task details, screenshots, replay steps, and local preferences are stored locally unless you choose to share them yourself.

Purchases are processed by our merchant-of-record provider, Lemon Squeezy. We receive only the information needed to fulfil a purchase and provide support, such as your name, email address, order identifier, and licence status. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Website data

This site does not use advertising cookies. Its appearance preference is saved locally in your browser if you switch themes. We may collect basic server logs needed to keep the site secure and reliable.
